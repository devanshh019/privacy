Privacy Policy

GAIN IG · Last updated: October 3, 2026

GAIN IG ("the app", "we") is a small project that connects to Instagram messaging through Meta's official Instagram API. This policy explains what data the app receives, how it is used, and how you can ask for it to be deleted.

Data we receive

When a user sends a direct message to the connected Instagram account, Instagram sends our server the following through a webhook:

  • The sender's Instagram-scoped user ID and, where provided, username
  • The content of the message (text and any attachment links)
  • Message metadata such as timestamps, message IDs, read receipts and reactions

For the connected Instagram account, we also hold an access token that lets the app read and send messages on that account's behalf.

We do not collect passwords, payment information, or your contacts.

How we use the data

  • To receive messages sent to the connected account and process them within the app
  • To send replies from the connected account
  • To debug and improve the app

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

We do not share message data with third parties, except service providers that host our server infrastructure, and where required by law. Data exchanged with Instagram is also subject to Meta's own privacy policy.

Your rights and data deletion

You can ask us to delete the data we hold about you at any time. Email devanshonline9087@gmail.com from any address with the subject "Delete my data" and include your Instagram username. We will delete the data linked to that account and confirm by reply within 30 days.

You can also remove the app's access to your Instagram account in Instagram → Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
